About Addiction

We're a streetwear store for people who live in their clothes. Here's who we are, what we sell and why we do it.

Who we are

Streetwear You'll Get Addicted To

Addiction is an online streetwear store. We sell three things, and we sell them well: graphic t-shirts, caps and jackets.

The name says it all. We want you to find that one piece you reach for every morning: the tee that fits just right, the cap that goes with everything, the jacket people ask you about. That feeling is what we design for.

Every product is picked for three things: how it looks, how it feels and how long it lasts. If we wouldn't wear it ourselves, it doesn't make it into the store.

Questions

Good to Know

Can't find your answer? Contact us and we'll get back to you.

How do I choose the right size?

Our tees come in regular and oversized fits. If you like a looser look, take your usual size in oversized. If you're between sizes, size up. Caps are adjustable and fit most heads.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery is free on every order over Rs 5,000. For smaller orders, a small delivery fee is shown at checkout before you pay.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Yes. You can return or exchange any unworn item with its tags within 30 days of delivery.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Right now we accept Cash on Delivery: you pay in cash when your order arrives. Card payments are coming soon.

How do I care for my clothes?

Wash printed tees inside out in cold water and hang them to dry. This keeps prints sharp and colours bright for longer.
