Privacy Policy

What information we collect, why we need it, and how we keep it safe.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Information we collect

  • When you place an order: your name, contact number, email address, delivery address, delivery notes and the items you order.
  • When you contact us: your name, email address and your message.
  • When you join our newsletter: your email address.

We do not collect card details. We currently accept Cash on Delivery only.

How we use it

  • To confirm, pack and deliver your order, and to call you about it.
  • To reply to your messages and help with returns or exchanges.
  • To send you news and offers, only if you signed up for them. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

  • Delivery partners, who need your name, phone and address to bring you your order.
  • FormSubmit, the service that sends your order and contact form details to our email inbox.

We never sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Your cart and browser storage

Your shopping cart is saved in your own browser so it's still there when you move between pages. It is not sent to us until you place an order. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Just contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
